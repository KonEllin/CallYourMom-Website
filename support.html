FAQ

Do I have to pick my mothers contact?

No you can pick any contact you wish!

How do I choose a contact?

Open Settings, tap Change contact, and pick someone from your contacts list.

Can I change the reminder schedule?

The app uses a fixed 24-hour check-in window.

Where is my data stored?

Your selected contact and check-ins stay locally on your device.

Will the app send a text?

If you miss a check-in for 48 hours, the app can send an automatic SMS update (sms/data rates may apply).

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app is designed to work without an account or internet connection.

How many contacts can I add?

The app currently supports one contact at a time.
