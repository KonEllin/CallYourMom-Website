A gentle nudge to call home.

Call Your Mom App keeps your family check-in streak alive with calm reminders, a clean check-in button, and just enough structure to make reaching out easy.

The simple flow

Choose your person

Pick Mom, Dad, or anyone you want to stay close to from your contacts.

Tap to check in

A single button marks your call for the day and keeps the streak alive.

Gentle reminders

Notifications escalate at 24 and 48 hours if you miss a check-in.

Automatic text update

After 48 hours without a check-in, the app can send a quick SMS update.

Stay on track

Review your last five check-ins and total count at a glance.
